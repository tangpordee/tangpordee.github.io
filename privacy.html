Privacy Policy — FB to YouTube

Last updated: 23 November 2026

FB to YouTube is a personal tool run by its owner only. It is not offered to other users.

What data it uses

  • Facebook: the videos, titles and post text of the owner's own Facebook Page, read through the Facebook Graph API.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, scope youtube.upload). The tool does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel or Google account.
  • AI titles: the post text is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to suggest a video title.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens and a list of already-uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's own computer or private storage. Downloaded video files are deleted right after upload. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

FB to YouTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
