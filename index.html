FB to YouTube

FB to YouTube is a personal tool used by the owner of the Facebook Page "สุขภาพดีวันละ1นาที" to copy that Page's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, with the same description and a suitable title.

It also sends the Page's short videos (1 to 4 minutes) to the owner's own TikTok account. Each video arrives in the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, and the owner reviews it, adds the suggested caption and posts it in the TikTok app.

It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.
